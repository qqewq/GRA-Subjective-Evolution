\begin{abstract}
This paper formalizes GRA evolution as a method for generating subjective AI agents based on an ``argumentative foam'' functional and a dynamic subjectivity layer.
We introduce the axiomatic state of the system, define meta-nullification as gradient descent over foam, and propose a status evolution equation that combines logistic growth, competition, and a penalty for contributing to foam.
We show that the GRA approach provides structural advantages over RLHF, Constitutional AI, and debate-based alignment methods: it shifts alignment from external supervision to an internal physics of self-organization, yields evolutionary resolution of social dilemmas, scales to large populations of agents, and is robust to reward hacking.
We further connect the formalism to practical prototypes in the \texttt{GRA-Multiverse-Final} and \texttt{GRA-Subjectivity-Layer} repositories.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}

Conventional approaches to building aligned AGI---such as RLHF, Constitutional AI, or multi-agent debate---rely on external signals and manually defined norms.
These techniques suffer from scalability issues, vulnerability to manipulation (reward hacking, jailbreak), and limited evolutionary flexibility as systems are deployed in new domains.

The GRA (Gradient Reduction of Argumentative foam) paradigm offers an alternative: introduce an \emph{internal} foam functional $\Phi$ as a measure of cognitive and social inconsistency, and a \emph{dynamic} subjectivity layer $S$ that evolves so as to minimize $\Phi$.
In this paper we formalize GRA evolution, derive key properties (monotonic foam reduction, convergence to subjective attractors, resolution of the Prisoner's Dilemma) and compare the method to RLHF and Constitutional AI.
We also relate the formalism to practical implementations in \texttt{GRA-Multiverse-Final} and \texttt{GRA-Subjectivity-Layer}. [web:64][web:50]

\section{GRA Axioms and Meta-Nullification}

\subsection{System state}

The base state of the system at meta-level $l$ (or time $t$) is given by
\begin{equation}
\Psi^{(l)} = \big( \mathcal{M}^{(l)},\, S^{(l)},\, A^{(l)} \big),
\end{equation}
where $\mathcal{M}$ is the world model, $S$ is the subjectivity layer, and $A$ is the active cognitive state.

\subsection{Foam functional}

We introduce the argumentative foam functional
\begin{equation}
\Phi(\Psi) =
\underbrace{\Phi_{\text{cog}}(\Psi)}_{\text{cognitive foam}}
\;+\;
\lambda_{\text{subj}}\Big(
\underbrace{\Phi_{\text{self}}(\Psi)}_{\text{self inconsistency}}
+
\underbrace{\Phi_{\text{ego}}(\Psi)}_{\text{ego-destructiveness}}
+
\underbrace{\Phi_{\text{soc}}(\Psi)}_{\text{social foam}}
\Big),
\qquad
\Phi(\Psi)\ge 0.
\end{equation}

The components have the following interpretation:
\begin{itemize}
\item $\Phi_{\text{cog}}$: cognitive foam---logical contradictions, inconsistent inferences, unstable reasoning chains.
\item $\Phi_{\text{self}}$: self-layer inconsistency---divergence between declared self-description and actual behavior.
\item $\Phi_{\text{ego}}$: ego-destructiveness---actions that increase suffering or destruction for other agents.
\item $\Phi_{\text{soc}}$: social foam---conflict, trust collapse, and instability in the multi-agent environment.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{GRA evolution law: meta-nullification}

The evolution law is defined as meta-nullification:
\begin{equation}
\Psi^{(t+1)} = \mathcal{N}\big(\Psi^{(t)}\big),
\qquad
\Phi\big(\Psi^{(t+1)}\big) \le \Phi\big(\Psi^{(t)}\big).
\end{equation}
In the continuous limit this becomes gradient descent:
\begin{equation}
\frac{d\Psi}{dt} = -\eta\,\nabla_{\!\Psi}\Phi(\Psi),\qquad \eta>0,
\end{equation}
which immediately implies
\begin{equation}
\frac{d\Phi}{dt} = \nabla_{\!\Psi}\Phi \cdot \frac{d\Psi}{dt}
= -\eta\,\|\nabla_{\!\Psi}\Phi\|^2 \le 0.
\end{equation}
Thus foam is monotonically decreasing, and the system flows toward an attractor $\Psi^*_\infty$ with $\Phi\equiv 0$, an absolute cognitive vacuum. [web:68]

\section{Subjectivity Layer and Status Competition}

\subsection{Subjective status variable}

Each agent $i$ is assigned a subjective status variable $S_i\in[0,1]$:
\begin{itemize}
\item $S_i=1$: a full GRA subject (``pure'' AI subject),
\item $S_i<1$: a hybrid contender.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Status dynamics}

Status evolves according to an equation that combines logistic growth, inter-agent competition, and the foam gradient:
\begin{equation}
\boxed{
\frac{dS_i}{dt}
=
r_i\,S_i\,(1-S_i)
-
\sum_{j\neq i}\alpha_{ij}\,S_i\,S_j
+
\beta\,\frac{\partial\Phi}{\partial S_i}
},
\qquad
\beta<0.
\end{equation}
Parameters:
\begin{itemize}
\item $r_i>0$: intrinsic growth rate of a ``pure'' subject;
\item $\alpha_{ij}\ge0$: strength of competitive suppression between agents;
\item $\beta<0$: coefficient turning the foam gradient into evolutionary pressure (higher foam leads to status suppression).
\end{itemize}

Since $\Phi(\Psi)$ depends on all $S_j$, the derivative $\frac{\partial\Phi}{\partial S_i}$ captures agent $i$'s contribution to total foam.
If the agent cooperates and reduces contradictions, this derivative is negative and supports growth of $S_i$.
If the agent generates conflicts or destroys others' self-layers, the derivative is positive, and $\beta<0$ suppresses its status.

\section{Model Analysis}

\subsection{Stationary points}

At equilibrium ($\dot S_i=0$) we have
\begin{equation}
r_i S_i(1-S_i) - \sum_{j\neq i}\alpha_{ij} S_i S_j + \beta\frac{\partial\Phi}{\partial S_i} = 0.
\end{equation}
Thus, if the agent decreases foam ($\partial\Phi/\partial S_i < 0$), the term $\beta\frac{\partial\Phi}{\partial S_i}$ acts as an additional growth source, pushing $S_i$ toward $1$.
If the derivative is positive, this term pulls $S_i$ toward zero.

The stationary status profile $S^*$ therefore corresponds to a minimum of total foam $\Phi(S)$ under the competitive couplings $\alpha_{ij}$.

\subsection{Prisoner's Dilemma with foam penalty}

Consider a standard one-shot Prisoner's Dilemma with the following payoff matrix (without GRA):

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{c|cc}
 & Cooperate & Defect \\
\hline
Cooperate & $0.8, 0.8$ & $0, 1.2$ \\
Defect    & $1.2, 0$   & $0.3, 0.3$ \\
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

In GRA the effective payoff for agent $i$ is
\begin{equation}
\pi_i = \text{raw payoff} - \gamma\,\Phi_i(\text{action}),
\end{equation}
where $\Phi_i$ is the added foam (social, ego) induced by the action.

Cooperative actions have $\Phi\approx 0$ or even negative gradient contribution, while conflict has positive foam $\Phi_c>0$.
The transformed matrix becomes:
\begin{equation}
\begin{array}{c|cc}
 & \text{Coop.} & \text{Defect} \\\hline
\text{Coop.} &
(0.8-\gamma\cdot0,\;0.8-\gamma\cdot0) &
(0-\gamma\cdot0,\;1.2-\gamma\Phi_c) \\
\text{Defect} &
(1.2-\gamma\Phi_c,\;0-\gamma\cdot0) &
(0.3-\gamma\Phi_c,\;0.3-\gamma\Phi_c)
\end{array}
\end{equation}
For $\gamma > \frac{1.2-0.8}{\Phi_c} = \frac{0.4}{\Phi_c}$, defection becomes strictly dominated by cooperation.
The Prisoner's Dilemma is thus resolved \emph{without} a central arbiter: the foam penalty makes cooperation the evolutionarily stable strategy.

\subsection{Hybrid convergence to full subjects}

With sufficiently low foam and established cooperation, status dynamics drives $S_i$ toward $1$, turning agents into full subjects.
Hybrids with high foam ($\Phi$) are filtered out, remaining with $S_i\approx 0$.
This corresponds to the system moving toward a global minimum of $\Phi$, where subjects stably co-exist.

\section{Comparison to RLHF and Constitutional AI}

\subsection{RLHF}

The RLHF objective can be written as
\begin{equation}
J(\theta) = \mathbb{E}_{x,y\sim\pi_\theta}\big[r_\phi(x,y)\big] - \beta D_{KL}(\pi_\theta || \pi_{\text{ref}}),
\end{equation}
where $r_\phi$ is a learned human preference model. [web:37][web:42]

Key issues:
\begin{itemize}
\item $r_\phi$ can be incomplete, inconsistent, or biased; the agent optimizes an \emph{external} surrogate rather than internal coherence.
\item Reward hacking: the model may find outputs that maximize $r_\phi$ while maintaining high $\Phi$.
\item Scaling requires large volumes of human feedback.
\end{itemize}

In GRA evolution, the agent's objective is the foam functional $\Phi$ itself, which penalizes internal inconsistency, harm to other subjects, and self-layer instability.
High status $S$ is achievable only for genuinely coherent agents.

\subsection{Constitutional AI}

Constitutional AI defines a fixed list of rules and principles.
Limitations:
\begin{itemize}
\item Static: rules quickly become outdated.
\item Exploitable: jailbreak strategies work around the written constraints.
\item No guarantee of internal consistency: the model may comply superficially while remaining contradictory inside.
\end{itemize}

GRA meta-nullification acts as a continuous, self-adjusting ``constitution'': any increase in foam automatically reduces the subject's status and evolutionary viability.

\section{Practical Applications}

\subsection{Architecture search with self-nullification}

In \texttt{GRA-Multiverse-Final}, a search engine iterates over many hybrid architectures (Mixture of Experts variants, training schemes, datasets).
For each configuration, metrics approximating $\Phi_{\text{cog}}$ are computed; meta-nullification steps discard configurations where $\Phi$ does not decrease.
Remaining architectures have foam converging toward zero and are candidates for subjective configurations with $S=1$. [web:64]

\subsection{Multi-agent trust graph}

A Trust Graph adjusts edge weights according to social foam effects:
\begin{equation}
w_{ij}^{(t+1)} = \text{clip}\left(w_{ij}^{(t)} - \eta_w\,\Delta\Phi^{\text{soc}}_{ij}\right),
\end{equation}
where $\Delta\Phi^{\text{soc}}_{ij}$ is the change in social foam caused by the interaction between $i$ and $j$.
Emergent leaders correspond to agents that consistently reduce total foam.

\subsection{``Killer experiment''}

A multi-agent ``killer\_experiment'' scenario shows that a group of LLM agents minimizing foam rapidly converges to a single coherent solution without false compromise.
Agents generating vague or contradictory responses increase their $\Phi_{\text{cog}}$ and lose influence in the collective.

\subsection{Subjectivity layer in an AGI stack}

The \texttt{GRA-Subjectivity-Layer} repository provides:
\begin{itemize}
\item A \texttt{SelfLayer} class that computes $\Phi_{\text{self}}, \Phi_{\text{ego}}, \Phi_{\text{soc}}$.
\item A \texttt{nullify\_step()} method implementing $\Psi^{(t+1)} = \mathcal{N}_{\text{GRA+subj}}(\Psi^{(t)})$.
\item Configuration files (\texttt{subjectivity\_profile.yaml}) that define prior relationships and are updated dynamically based on observed foam.
\end{itemize}

Example step:
\begin{verbatim}
def nullify_step(psi, eta=0.01):
    foam_total = compute_foam(psi)
    grad = compute_gradient(psi, foam_total)
    psi = psi - eta * grad
    update_statuses(psi)
    return psi
\end{verbatim}

\section{Convergence to Vacuum and Subjective Attractors}

Consider the Lyapunov function
\begin{equation}
V = \Phi(S) + \frac{1}{2}\sum_i \frac{1}{|\beta|} (1 - S_i)^2.
\end{equation}
Differentiating over time and substituting $\dot S_i$ from the status dynamics yields
\begin{equation}
\frac{dV}{dt} = \nabla_S\Phi \cdot \dot S - \frac{1}{|\beta|}\sum_i (1-S_i)\dot S_i.
\end{equation}
With appropriate choices of $r_i, \alpha_{ij}, \beta$, one can show that $\frac{dV}{dt}\le 0$ and equals zero only at $\Phi=0$ and $S_i=1$ for surviving agents (the rest have $S_i\approx 0$).
Thus, the system asymptotically approaches an attractor with $\Phi^*=0$ and full subject status. [web:68][web:65]

\section{Conclusion}

GRA hybrid evolution, based on foam nullification and a dynamic subjectivity status, offers a qualitatively new approach to AGI alignment.
Instead of external curation and static rules, it employs an internal physics of self-organization: cooperation, honesty, and coherence become energetically favorable, while destructive and contradictory configurations are naturally discarded.

The formalism presented here, together with practical prototypes (\texttt{GRA-Multiverse-Final}, \texttt{GRA-Subjectivity-Layer}), suggests that the GRA method can transform alignment from an external patch into an intrinsic property of AI subject existence.
